about

Get Keke Out the Club! is a browser game controlled by sound. Keke is in a hurry, and the player steers her sideways through a neon dance floor using a microphone, or a contact mic you tap on a CD. The louder the sound, the further right she goes, and she has to dodge obstacles like an ex and a spilled drink to reach the exit. The game is built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript and measures sound with the Web Audio API. We are NAMN and NAMN, and we made it as our final project in KURSNAMN.
