01 / THE PROJECT

ABOUT THE GAME

**Get Keke Out the Club!** is an interactive game where the player has to help Keke escape the club by using sound. By making noise near the microphone, the player controls Keke's movement from side to side while avoiding incoming obstacles. The game explores how sound can be transformed into movement in a playful and unexpected way. As a creator, I enjoy designing fun and engaging experiences that reflect my personality. I like experimenting with visual styles, creating playful interactions and making people smile or laugh. My goal is to create designs that invite people to participate, explore and have fun.
